About Prowire Electric

Electrical work, from planning to final testing.

Prowire Electric supports residential and commercial new builds, renovations, upgrades, and specialty systems across Squamish and the Sea to Sky corridor. Project communication is centred on scope, safety, scheduling, and clean installation.

Our Process

How projects move from consultation to testing.

Prowire works with homeowners, builders, and commercial clients through the main project stages so scope, budget, schedule, and installation details are clear before the job is finished.

01

Electrical Design

The electrical design is planned around the property's needs, new-build or renovation scope, safety requirements, budget, and applicable electrical codes.

02

On-Site Consultations

On-site consultations help identify the right options before the work starts, with the plan shaped around the client's budget and priorities.

03

Installation and Construction

Electricians install and construct electrical systems using project-appropriate materials and equipment.

04

Finishing Touches and Testing

Once installation is complete, the team tests the system and handles the final checks against applicable safety and regulatory standards.
