Common questions

Electrical service questions.

These answers help homeowners, builders, and businesses prepare for an estimate or consultation.

Does Prowire take on new builds?

Yes. Prowire supports residential and commercial new builds from electrical planning and rough-in through panels, finishing, specialty systems, and final testing.

How is an electrical project priced?

Pricing is based on scope, materials, existing service capacity, access, permitting requirements, and schedule. Project details help produce a more useful estimate.

Where does Prowire work?

Prowire is based in Squamish and serves the Sea to Sky corridor, including Whistler, Vancouver, and surrounding communities depending on the project.

Can you install a home EV charger?

Yes. The installation process considers charger placement, a dedicated circuit, panel capacity, load requirements, and safe operation.

Do you work on renovations and additions?

Yes. Residential services include renovation and addition wiring, lighting, outlets, panels, upgrades, troubleshooting, and coordination with the wider project.

Can Prowire coordinate with builders and other trades?

Yes. Project planning can account for construction schedules, access, equipment placement, finishing requirements, and coordination with the wider project team.
